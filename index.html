Local AI — Pocket Node

Run a private AI agent on the phone you already own. Not a chatbot: an agent that reads, writes and surgically edits files in your own notes vault, entirely on-device, and keeps working in airplane mode.

Status: M0 — validation spike

Not ready to follow yet. Nothing here is a setup guide. This stage exists to test the handful of assumptions the design rests on, on a real phone, before any of it gets built. The toolkit, the vault template and the session materials come next — and deliberately not before the probes come back.
